开始一次学习

打开 QuietStudy，左右滑动选择一个场景，然后点按屏幕中央的 Start。计时从点按的那一刻开始，只累计学习时间。

暂停、继续与结束

怎么暂停？

学习时轻点画面任意处即可暂停。暂停时间不计入净学习时长。

怎么继续？

在暂停页轻点播放图标，或点按页面空白处，都会继续当前会话。

怎么结束？

在暂停页点按 End，会话立即结算并回到首页。不足一分钟的会话不会保存。

场景与声音

可以换场景吗？

可以。学习时左右滑动即可切换场景，当前会话与计时不会重置。每个场景由一张静态画面与一段可循环的环境声组成，全部随应用打包，离线可用。

怎么调音量？

在画面上长按约四分之一秒，然后上下拖动。滑杆会出现在按压点的另一侧，避免手指挡住读数。上滑增大，下滑减小。

为什么没有声音？

先确认「设置 → 声音 → 环境声音」是开启状态，再检查系统媒体音量。环境声使用媒体音量播放，不受静音开关影响。如果连接了耳机或蓝牙设备，声音会从当前输出设备播放。

试用与完整版

首次打开 QuietStudy 会开始 3 天免费试用，期间全部场景与功能可用。试用结束后，森林场景继续免费；解锁其余场景与完整功能需要一次买断。价格以 App Store 显示为准，没有订阅，不会自动续费，试用期间不会扣费。可以在付费墙或「设置 → 完整版」恢复购买。

统计

从「设置 → 统计」进入，分概览、趋势与记录三段：概览看今日、本周、本月与累计，趋势看日柱状图、时段与单次时长分布，记录用于回看每次会话。统计只保存在本机。

锁屏与灵动岛

学习时会话会以只读实时活动显示在锁屏上，包含场景、净时长与暂停状态。若没有显示，请确认系统设置允许实时活动；灵动岛需要 iPhone 14 Pro 及以上机型。关闭实时活动不影响计时与统计。

数据与隐私

QuietStudy 不需要账号，不收集数据，不发起网络请求。学习记录与偏好只保存在本机。「设置 → 数据 → 清空学习记录」可以删除全部会话，设置项保持不变。

Support

Most questions are answered below. If not, scroll to the contact section.

Starting a session

Open QuietStudy, swipe left or right to choose a scene, then tap Start in the centre of the screen. The timer starts at that moment and counts only study time.

Pause, resume and end

How do I pause?

While studying, tap anywhere on the scene to pause. Paused time is not included in your net study duration.

How do I resume?

On the paused screen, tap the play icon or tap any empty area to continue the same session.

How do I end?

Tap End on the paused screen. The session is saved immediately and you return to the home screen. Sessions shorter than one minute are not saved.

Scenes and sound

Can I change scenes?

Yes. Swipe left or right while studying; the current session and timer keep running. Each scene pairs a still image with a looping ambient sound, bundled inside the app and available offline.

How do I adjust the volume?

Long-press the scene for about a quarter of a second, then drag up or down. The slider appears on the opposite side of your finger. Drag up to increase, down to decrease.

Why is there no sound?

Check that Settings → Sound → Environment sound is on, then check your system media volume. Ambient sound uses media volume and is not affected by the silent switch. If headphones or Bluetooth are connected, sound plays through that device.

Trial and full version

Your first launch starts a 3-day free trial with every scene and feature available. When the trial ends, the Forest scene stays free; unlocking the remaining scenes and the full app requires a one-time purchase. The price is shown by the App Store. There is no subscription, nothing renews automatically, and the trial never charges you. You can restore a purchase from the paywall or Settings → Full version.

Statistics

Open Settings → Statistics. Overview shows today, this week, this month and all time; Trends shows a daily chart, time of day and session length; Records lets you review each session. Everything stays on this device.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

During a session, a read-only Live Activity shows the scene, net duration and pause state on the Lock Screen. If it does not appear, check that Live Activities are allowed in system settings. Dynamic Island requires iPhone 14 Pro or later. Turning it off does not affect timing or statistics.

Data and privacy

QuietStudy needs no account, collects no data and makes no network requests. Study records and preferences stay on this device. Settings → Data → Clear study records removes all sessions while keeping your preferences.
