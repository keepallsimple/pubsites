闹钟没有响

先看这三个开关

打开 iOS「设置 → 通知 → Mosolis」，确认允许通知；再进入 Mosolis 设置页确认系统闹钟权限已开启；最后检查闹钟本身的开关是否是开启状态。

App 被关掉还会响吗？

会。每条闹钟都会注册为 iOS 系统闹钟，App 退到后台或被关闭后仍由系统负责响铃。若你在系统设置里撤销了闹钟权限，需要重新打开 App 让它重新注册。

为什么离开告警界面后声音停了？

这是 iOS 的行为：离开系统闹钟的告警界面会结束这次响铃。请使用界面上的「稍后提醒」或「停止」。

声音与震动

听不到唤醒声

先确认媒体音量不是最低，并检查专注模式与静音开关。八条声景都打包在 App 内，不依赖网络，离线也能播放。

可以换成系统铃声或单独设置震动吗？

iOS 不允许第三方闹钟使用系统铃声，也没有开放单独的震动接口。选择「无声音」时，是否震动由系统的声音与触感设置决定。

倒计时

常用的时长会显示为圆圈，点一下即开始；运行时可以加减五分钟，结束时还有几秒可以撤销。倒计时只在 App 内启动，不会写入闹钟列表。

试用、买断与恢复购买

七天试用包含什么？

七天内所有外观、所有声景和所有闹钟功能都开放。到期后一次购买永久解锁，没有订阅，也不会自动续费。

换了设备或重装了 App

在 Mosolis 设置页点「恢复购买」，使用购买时的同一个 Apple ID 即可恢复。试用计时保存在本机，重装后会重新计算。

外观

设置页提供跟随系统、深色、纯黑、浅色四档外观，以及苔绿、暖米、灰蓝三套色温。桌面小组件与实时活动跟随系统外观，不单独跟随 App 内的外观设置。

Support

Most questions are answered below. If not, scroll to the contact section.

The alarm did not ring

Check these three switches

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Mosolis and allow notifications. Then open Mosolis Settings and make sure alarm permission is on. Finally confirm the alarm's own switch is enabled.

Will it ring if the app is closed?

Yes. Each alarm is registered as a system alarm with iOS, so it rings even after the app is closed. If you revoke alarm permission in system settings, open the app again so alarms can be registered.

Why did the sound stop after leaving the alert screen?

That is how iOS behaves: leaving the system alarm alert ends that ring. Use Snooze or Stop on the alert screen.

Sound and vibration

I cannot hear the wake sound

Check that media volume is up and that Focus or the silent switch is not blocking sound. All eight nature scenes are bundled in the app and play offline.

Can I use a system ringtone or set vibration separately?

iOS does not allow third-party alarm apps to use system ringtones, and it exposes no separate vibration API. With No sound selected, vibration follows your system Sound & Haptics settings.

Countdown

Saved durations appear as circles and start with one tap. You can add or remove five minutes while running and undo for a few seconds after ending. Countdowns start inside the app and are never added to your alarm list.

Trial, purchase and restore

What is included in the seven-day trial?

Every appearance, every nature scene and every alarm feature is open for seven days. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks Mosolis for good. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal.

I changed devices or reinstalled the app

Tap Restore Purchases in Mosolis Settings while signed in with the same Apple ID. The trial timer is stored on the device and starts again after reinstalling.

Appearance

Settings offers System, Dark, Pure black and Light appearance, with three colour temperatures: moss, warm sand and mist. The Home Screen widget and Live Activity follow the system appearance rather than the in-app setting.
