隐私政策

QuietStudy · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 28 日

简体中文 English 使用支持

QuietStudy 不要求账号，不收集或传输个人数据，也不使用广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

应用在设备上保存什么

为提供学习计时与统计，QuietStudy 会在你的设备上保存每次学习会话的开始与结束时间、净学习时长、场景、暂停区间，以及默认场景、环境声音开关、音量与画面模式等偏好。锁屏实时活动只读取当前会话的场景与净时长。

数据如何使用与传输

应用不会把上述数据发送给开发者，也不接入第三方分析或广告服务。核心功能不依赖网络，媒体资源全部打包在应用内。iOS 可能会根据你的系统设置把应用数据纳入设备备份，备份由 Apple 的系统服务和你的设备设置管理。内购交易与收据由 Apple 处理，应用不接触你的支付信息。

系统权限

QuietStudy 不申请相机、相册、通讯录、定位、麦克风或通知权限。环境声使用系统音频会话在应用内播放；锁屏实时活动由 iOS 管理，你可以在系统设置中关闭，不影响计时与统计。

删除数据

你可以在应用内「设置 → 数据 → 清空学习记录」删除本机上的全部学习会话，设置项不受影响。删除应用会移除设备上的应用数据，但系统备份中可能仍保留副本，具体由你的备份设置决定。

儿童隐私

QuietStudy 不收集任何个人信息，因此不会通过应用收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新与联系

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策会相应更新。对隐私有疑问，写信到 truebraveheart@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

QuietStudy does not require an account and does not collect, transmit or track personal data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

To provide the study timer and statistics, QuietStudy stores each session's start and end time, net study duration, scene, pause intervals, and preferences such as the default scene, environment sound, volume and display mode. The Lock Screen Live Activity reads only the current session's scene and net duration.

How data is used and shared

The app does not send this data to the developer and does not include third-party analytics or advertising. Core features work without a network connection, and all media is bundled inside the app. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your system settings. In-app purchases and receipts are handled by Apple; the app never receives your payment details.

System permissions

QuietStudy does not request Camera, Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone or notification access. Ambient sound plays inside the app using the system audio session. Live Activities are managed by iOS and can be turned off in system settings without affecting the timer or statistics.

Deleting data

You can remove all study sessions on this device in Settings → Data → Clear study records; preferences stay unchanged. Deleting the app removes its data from the device, although system backups may keep a copy depending on your backup settings.

Children's privacy

QuietStudy does not collect personal information, so it does not collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy questions, write to truebraveheart@gmail.com.
