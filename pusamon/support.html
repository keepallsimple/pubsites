PUSAMON · 常见问题

简体中文 English

PUSAMON 是一个本地使用的暂停空间，不需要注册或联网。

开始使用

如何开始一次暂停？

在首页点击“I need a pause”，选择体验方式和可选时长。你可以随时退出，计时结束也不会自动结束体验。

体验内容

为什么自然、生命或声音体验暂不可用？

这些体验需要经过授权的媒体内容。目前相应素材仍在准备中，因此部分页面会显示准备提示。内容就绪后会随应用更新提供。

记录与收藏

暂停记录和收藏存在哪里？

它们保存在本机，不需要账号。可在“设置 → 隐私与数据”清除记录与收藏；主题、语言和个人意图会保留。

联系支持

PUSAMON 的公开支持邮箱尚未提供。联系方式会在公开发行前补充；在此之前，可查看应用内“设置 → 帮助与支持”。

Support

PUSAMON is an on-device space to pause. It does not require an account or an internet connection.

Getting started

How do I start a pause?

Tap “I need a pause” on the home screen, then choose an experience and an optional duration. You can leave at any time; the timer does not end the experience automatically.

Experiences

Why are Nature, Living, or Sound unavailable?

These experiences require properly licensed media. The assets are still being prepared, so some screens show an availability note. They will be offered in an app update when ready.

Records and favorites

Where are my pause records and favorites stored?

They stay on your device and do not require an account. Clear them in Settings → Privacy & Data. Theme, language, and intention preferences remain.

Contact support

A public support email for PUSAMON has not been provided yet. Contact details will be added before public release. For now, see Settings → Help & Support in the app.
