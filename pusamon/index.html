当你想停一下，PUSAMON 为冲动与行动之间留出一点空间。无需解释，也不必完成任何事。

给自己一个暂停

从呼吸、文字或安静留白中，选择此刻适合你的方式。你可以随时离开，也可以多停留一会儿。

自然影像、生命片刻与环境声音仍在准备中；正式提供前，页面会保持清楚的提示，不会播放未获授权的内容。

A moment to pause

When you need a moment, PUSAMON makes room between impulse and action. No explanation or task required.

Choose what feels right: a gentle breathing visual, a thought to sit with, or stillness. Leave whenever you like, or stay a little longer.

Nature videos, living moments, and ambient audio are still being prepared. Until licensed content is available, the app clearly indicates that these experiences are not ready.
