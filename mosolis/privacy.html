隐私政策

Mosolis · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

简体中文 English 使用支持

Mosolis 不要求账号，不收集或传输个人数据，也不使用广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

应用在设备上保存什么

为提供闹钟功能，Mosolis 会在你的设备上保存闹钟的时间、重复日、名称、唤醒声、开关状态，倒计时场景，外观与色温偏好，试用起始日期，以及桌面小组件所需的闹钟快照。这些数据由应用在本地使用。

数据如何使用与传输

应用不会把上述数据发送给开发者，也不接入第三方分析或广告服务。核心功能不依赖网络。购买与恢复购买由 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理，交易信息由 Apple 管理，开发者不会获取你的支付卡信息。iOS 可能会根据你的系统设置把应用数据纳入设备备份，备份由 Apple 的系统服务和你的设备设置管理。

系统权限

Mosolis 需要闹钟权限，才能把闹钟注册为 iOS 系统闹钟并在设定时间响铃。拒绝权限时，应用仍可浏览与编辑闹钟，但闹钟不会响。应用不申请通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风或健康数据权限。

删除数据

你可以在应用内删除闹钟与倒计时场景，并在设置中切换或重置外观偏好。删除应用会移除设备上的应用数据，但系统备份中可能仍保留副本，具体由你的备份设置决定。

儿童隐私

Mosolis 不收集任何个人信息，因此不会通过应用收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新与联系

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策会相应更新。对隐私有疑问，写信到 truebraveheart@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Mosolis does not require an account and does not collect, transmit or track personal data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

To run alarms, Mosolis stores each alarm's time, repeat days, name, wake sound, on/off state, countdown scenes, appearance and colour-temperature preferences, the trial start date, and the alarm snapshot used by the Home Screen widget. This data is used locally by the app.

How data is used and shared

The app does not send this data to the developer and does not include third-party analytics or advertising. Core features work without a network connection. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple's StoreKit, and Apple manages transaction information; the developer never receives your payment card details. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your system settings.

System permission

Mosolis needs alarm permission to register alarms with iOS and ring at the time you set. If permission is denied, you can still browse and edit alarms, but they will not ring. The app does not request Contacts, Location, Camera, Microphone or Health access.

Deleting data

You can delete alarms and countdown scenes inside the app and change or reset appearance preferences in Settings. Deleting the app removes its data from the device, although system backups may keep a copy depending on your backup settings.

Children's privacy

Mosolis does not collect personal information, so it does not collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy questions, write to truebraveheart@gmail.com.
