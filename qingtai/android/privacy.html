Privacy Policy

QingTai · Android / Huawei AppGallery · Effective 28 November 2026

阅读中文版

QingTai collects no personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. All sounds are stored on your device, and the app is fully usable without a network connection.

1. What we do not collect

  • No registration and no account system.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, location, or health data.
  • No data sent to any server. This build declares no network permission.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

2. What is stored on your device

To restore your last setup, the app stores the following locally using Android SharedPreferences:

  • The selected entry, automatic or manual.
  • Volume and loop mode.
  • Play duration and fade-out duration.
  • Night brightness and whether the drag hint has been shown.
  • The last played track.

This data is readable only by the app on your device. It never leaves the device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Third parties and audio licences

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and this build contains no in-app purchases.

Two instrumental tracks (Rest Now, Voxscape) and other sound effects come from Mixkit under the Mixkit Free License. Some nature recordings come from the United States National Park Service public-domain collection. The fan sound is by 1jmorrisoncafe291 (Freesound), licensed under CC BY 3.0, source freesound.org/s/121307; it has been trimmed, hard-looped, gain-adjusted, and transcoded.

4. Children

QingTai is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect age information. Because no data is collected, there is no processing of children's data.

5. Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete, export, or correct on our side. You can remove all locally stored settings and state by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes

If a future version adds a feature that requires a network connection, this page will be updated before that version takes effect and the app will say so. This page is not fetched from a server. The current version has no network features.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: truebraveheart@gmail.com.

隐私政策（中文）

青苔 · Android / 华为应用市场 · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 28 日

青苔不收集任何个人信息。没有账号，没有统计，没有广告，也没有服务器。所有声音都在设备里，App 在没有网络时功能完整。

一、我们不收集什么

  • 不要求注册，也没有账号系统。
  • 不接入任何统计、崩溃上报或广告 SDK。
  • 不读取通讯录、照片、麦克风、位置、健康数据。
  • 不向任何服务器发送数据；此版本不声明联网权限。
  • 不做跨 App 或跨网站追踪。

二、设备上存了什么

为了让下次打开还是你上次的样子，App 使用 Android SharedPreferences 在本机保存以下播放设置与界面状态：

  • 选中的入口 —— 自动或手动选择。
  • 音量与循环方式。
  • 播放时长与渐弱时长。
  • 夜间亮度，以及是否已显示拖动提示。
  • 上次播放的曲目。

这些只有本机能读，不会离开设备。删掉 App 就一起删掉了。

三、第三方与声音授权

App 内没有第三方统计或广告 SDK；此版本不含任何内购。

两条器乐（Rest Now、Voxscape）与其余音效来自 Mixkit，按 Mixkit Free License 使用。部分自然录音取自美国国家公园管理局的公共领域声库。风扇声由 1jmorrisoncafe291（Freesound）创作，许可为 CC BY 3.0，来源 freesound.org/s/121307；已做裁剪、硬循环、增益调整与转码。

四、儿童

青苔不面向 13 岁以下儿童单独设计，也不收集任何年龄信息。因为不收集任何数据，也就不存在儿童数据的处理问题。

五、你的权利

由于我们不持有你的任何数据，删除、导出、更正这些权利在技术上没有对象。你能做的操作是删掉 App，本机保存的设置与状态会一并清除。

六、政策变更

如果将来加入需要联网的功能，本页会在生效前更新，并在 App 内提示。本页不从服务器拉取。当前版本没有任何联网功能。

七、联系

对本政策有疑问，写信到 truebraveheart@gmail.com。
