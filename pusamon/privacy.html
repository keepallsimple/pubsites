隐私政策

PUSAMON · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

简体中文 English

PUSAMON 不要求账号，不收集或传输个人数据，也不使用广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

应用在设备上保存什么

为提供应用功能，PUSAMON 会在你的设备上保存暂停记录、收藏、主题、语言和个人意图。这些数据由应用本地使用。

数据如何使用与传输

应用不将上述数据发送给 PUSAMON，也不接入第三方分析或广告服务。iOS 可能会根据你的系统设置，将应用数据纳入设备备份；备份由 Apple 的系统服务和你的设备设置管理。

删除数据

你可以在应用的“设置 → 隐私与数据”中清除暂停记录和收藏。主题、语言和个人意图偏好会保留。删除应用会移除设备上的应用数据，但系统备份可能仍保留备份副本。

儿童隐私

PUSAMON 不收集个人信息，因此不会通过应用收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新与联系

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策会相应更新。PUSAMON 的公开联系邮箱尚未提供；发布前会在此页补充联系方式。

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

PUSAMON requires no account and does not collect or transmit personal data. It uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

What the app stores

To provide its features, PUSAMON stores pause records, favorites, theme, language, and intention on your device for local use.

Use and transmission

The app does not send this information to PUSAMON and does not use third-party analytics or advertising services. Depending on your system settings, iOS may include app data in device backups. Those backups are managed by Apple system services and your device settings.

Deleting data

You can clear pause records and favorites in Settings → Privacy & Data. Theme, language, and intention preferences remain. Deleting the app removes its data from the device, though system backups may retain a copy.

Children’s privacy

PUSAMON does not collect personal information, including personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. A public contact email for PUSAMON has not been provided yet and will be added here before release.
